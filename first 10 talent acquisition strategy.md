# First 10 Talent Acquisition Strategy

Date: 2026-03-06

## Core Rule

Do not try to build a large roster early.

The goal is not volume.
The goal is to get the first `1-10` represented faces who make the model believable, premium, and operationally testable.

## Should You Include Yourself?

Yes, if:

- you fit the visual and strategic positioning
- you are comfortable being a represented face
- you want to pressure-test the workflow firsthand

Using yourself early can help because:

- you control approvals
- you can test rights and restriction logic
- you can generate proof faster
- you reduce dependency on outside talent in the first phase

But:

- do not let the brand become your personal portfolio
- your presence should validate the system, not dominate it

## What The First 10 Should Look Like

Aim for:

- `1-3` to start
- `5` when the system feels real
- `10` only once quality and process stay strong

Best first-profile characteristics:

- strong editorial or campaign presence
- distinct face and image value
- cultural relevance
- multi-hyphenate identity where useful
- comfort with commercial digital use
- trust in you and the model

Do not optimize for:

- high follower counts alone
- generic model-board looks
- people who only want exposure
- people who do not understand the implications of digital likeness

## Best Early Talent Sources

1. Yourself
2. Friends and trusted collaborators
3. DJs, producers, artists, and creatives already in your network
4. Models with strong editorial value
5. Culturally relevant people who are underrepresented commercially but visually strong

## Best Talent Profile Types For Sembla

Early Sembla talent should skew toward:

- DJs
- producers
- artists
- performers
- image-led creatives
- models or supermodels with broader brand value

These profiles are often better than generic fashion-only talent because they:

- carry stronger cultural signal
- make brand campaigns more distinctive
- have more long-term likeness value

## How To Approach Them

Do not pitch this as:

- free AI headshots
- exposure
- a vague future opportunity

Pitch it as:

- represented digital rights
- controlled brand access
- new revenue from governed commercial use
- protection from uncontrolled AI use

## First Outreach Script Logic

The message should say:

- why them specifically
- what Sembla is
- what would be controlled
- what they would approve
- how they would make money
- why this is better than unmanaged AI use

## What They Need To Believe

For someone to join early, they need to believe:

- they stay in control
- they can approve or reject uses
- this is not a free-for-all avatar play
- this can create new, premium revenue
- their image will not be cheapened

## What You Need Before Signing Anyone

Before adding even the first few people, have:

- a clear consent structure
- category restrictions
- approval logic
- payment or revenue-share logic
- a simple likeness passport record
- a defined inquiry path for brands

Without this, the roster becomes aesthetic before it becomes trustworthy.

## Recommended First 10 Mix

If you eventually reach ten, a strong mix might be:

- `2-3` high-image-value models
- `2-3` DJs / producers
- `2` artists or performers
- `1-2` multi-hyphenate cultural figures
- `1` founder-led or in-house proof profile if appropriate

The point is range with coherence, not randomness.

## Selection Criteria

Only include someone if at least most of these are true:

- they have strong campaign presence
- they are visually distinctive
- they understand consent and digital rights
- they trust the process
- they could plausibly interest premium brands
- they strengthen the roster's cultural signal

## What To Avoid

Avoid:

- open applications too early
- “DM for representation” energy
- overfilling the roster with weak profiles
- mixing real represented people with synthetic figures on the same public board

## Best Sequence

1. Add yourself if appropriate.
2. Add `1-2` trusted people from your network.
3. Build one proof case with them.
4. Use that proof to approach slightly higher-value talent.
5. Keep quality high and roster size low.

## North Star

The first 10 should not just be available people.

They should be:

`faces whose likeness is valuable enough to govern.`
