# Sembla Simulated Beta Test: Named Accounts

Date: 2026-03-06

## Important Note

This is still a simulation, not real user research.

The named people below are current public leaders or likely executive sponsors, not proof that these individuals have used Sembla.
The scoring is an inferred fit score based on:

- the current Sembla site and offer stack
- each brand's public positioning
- each company's known investment in premium brand systems, digital marketing, partnerships, and controlled global rollout

Use this as a target-account map.

## Core Conclusion

Sembla aligns best with premium brands that need all four of these at once:

- represented talent or ambassador access
- clear rights and approval logic
- repeatable campaign output across markets
- strong control over reuse, image, and brand risk

That is why the strongest current fit remains:

- luxury fashion
- beauty
- luxury hospitality
- premium spirits

Music-adjacent is real, but it is still better treated as an expansion lane than a top-line public vertical.

## How To Read This

- `9-10/10` = high-probability fit right now
- `7-8/10` = strong expansion fit
- `4-6/10` = conditional or selective fit
- `1-3/10` = low-fit / not-for-now

## What This Means For Positioning

The current site should stay anchored to premium brand buyers, not broaden into generic entertainment or creator tooling.

The best public framing remains:

- luxury fashion
- beauty
- luxury hospitality
- premium spirits

Music-adjacent should stay in the background for now as:

- cultural partnerships
- ambassador activations
- live or event-led brand extensions

That keeps the demand-side story clean while preserving a larger future market.

## Real Buying Roles Under The Named Sponsors

The people who would actually move first are often not the CEO-level names above.
In practice, the first serious conversations are most likely to come from:

- brand marketing leads
- campaign directors
- chief marketing officers
- digital and innovation leaders
- commercial and partnerships teams
- legal / rights / business affairs stakeholders

## Sharpest Current ICP

If Sembla had to choose the single clearest ICP now, it would be:

`global or regional brand, campaign, or innovation leaders at premium fashion, beauty, hospitality, and spirits brands that need controlled, rights-cleared digital use of high-value talent.`

## What Sembla Is Not

Sembla is not best positioned today as:

- a self-serve AI content tool
- a generic creator marketplace
- a fast-fashion performance engine
- a mass-influencer booking platform
- a low-cost avatar studio
