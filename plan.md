At least one case study should remain a standing requirement for the site and decks.

Best first case study:

- sector: beauty
- format: launch campaign across three markets
- focus: represented talent, defined rights, repeatable outputs, renewal governance

Best structure:

- Challenge
- Structure
- Outputs
- Governance

The case study should be:

- redacted enough to stay private
- specific enough to build trust
- commercially legible, not editorial fluff

## Do Not Lose Focus

Do not expand into:

- generic AI content studio work
- public pricing
- self-serve product demos
- marketplace positioning

The north stars remain:

- premium talent access
- clear rights
- repeatable commercial use
- approvals and governance attached
