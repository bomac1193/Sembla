# Sembla

**Face Value. Zero Commission.**

## Diagnosis

The creator economy has a new asset class: AI-generated avatars derived from personal likenesses. But there's no trusted marketplace for them. Models don't know what their synthetic face is worth. Brands can't confidently license AI avatars without clear consent chains. There's no standardized way to track provenance, verify rights, or attribute campaigns.

The result is a fragmented market where creators undervalue their likeness, brands operate in legal grey zones, and everyone loses the audit trail the moment an image leaves the platform.

## Guiding Policy

Sembla operates on **consent-as-code**. Every avatar generated carries an immutable consent record—a signed JSON document capturing the signer's name, email, timestamp, rights granted, and campaign attribution. This consent travels with the asset.

The economic model is radically transparent: **zero commission**. Models set their own rates and receive same-day settlement. Every output is watermarked and assigned a unique license token that enables tracking, revocation, and legal backing.

## Strategy

### 1. Auditable Provenance

Every generated avatar is stored with its full context: the original selfie, consent JSON, generation parameters, license token, and campaign source (via QR attribution). This creates a complete chain-of-custody that satisfies both regulatory requirements and brand safety concerns. Nothing is generated without explicit, machine-readable consent.

### 2. Direct-to-Market Economics

No intermediaries between models and brands. Models upload, set rates, and get paid directly. Brands browse the roster, select avatars by license token, and book through a direct channel. The platform facilitates the transaction but takes nothing from it—revenue comes from enterprise features, not creator exploitation.
