# Opportunities

## Brand Deliverables

When a brand books through Sembla, the deliverable should not be framed as "images."
It should be framed as usable likeness with commercial clarity.

Core deliverables:

- Booking memo: talent selected, campaign scope, territory, term, media, exclusivity, revision rounds
- Consent and approval pack: signed likeness consent, approved use cases, restrictions, approval trail
- Creative pack: references, styling direction, prompt direction, brand guardrails
- Asset delivery: final stills or video renders, aspect ratios, cutdowns, selects, export files
- License reference: profile or licensing ID used for approvals and future requests
- Governance handoff: provenance record, consent record, usage summary, takedown or change path

## Offer Structure

Sembla should be positioned as an agency offer rather than a generic AI tool.

Recommended structure:

1. Campaign booking
   Rights-cleared deliverables for a defined campaign scope
2. Digital twin build
   Capture, training, voice, likeness setup, approvals, and guardrails
3. Twin management
   Ongoing refreshes, new looks, localization, usage governance, access control

## Digital Twin Opportunity

Sembla should offer digital twin creation, but as a premium add-on rather than the default booking.

Best fit:

- recurring campaigns
- spokesperson or presenter work
- multilingual rollout
- interactive or always-on brand use

Less suitable:

- one-off still campaigns
- clients who only need a small approved asset set quickly

## Positioning

The strategic edge is not "AI avatar production."
The strategic edge is rights-cleared representation plus twin creation.

This means the core value proposition is:

- represented talent
- documented consent
- clear commercial usage
- controlled delivery

## Recommendation

Short term:

- sell campaign bookings first
- keep digital twin work as a premium second-line offer
- emphasize governance, discretion, and rights clarity

Long term:

- develop digital twin builds into a distinct service line
- support them with stronger approval, access, and usage controls
